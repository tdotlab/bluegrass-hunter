# Research rubric

## Website classification

### No discoverable owned website

Searching the business name, town, phone number, and category does not reveal a credible
business-owned domain. Social profiles and directory listings do not count as an owned site.

### Weak website

A domain exists, but the site has obvious problems such as broken pages, poor mobile layout,
unclear services, weak contact flow, stale information, poor local relevance, or visibly dated design.

### Adequate website

The site is reachable, understandable, mobile-usable, and provides a credible path to contact or book.

### Unclear

The evidence is conflicting or too thin to classify responsibly.

## Opportunity score

- 5: clear business, high-value service, no/weak site, strong local presence
- 4: good opportunity with one meaningful uncertainty
- 3: plausible but ordinary lead
- 2: weak fit, weak evidence, or likely low urgency
- 1: do not spend time on it

The score is a prioritization aid, not a prediction of whether the owner will buy.
